The problem

Finding a boarding place still runs on Facebook posts and phone calls.

It works, eventually. But it costs students and owners a lot of time, and a lot of the information is out of date by the time you see it.

  • Adverts that outlive the room

    Posts stay up long after a room is taken, so you only find out when you call.

  • Hard to compare

    Every listing is written differently, in a different group, with different details missing.

  • Prices you have to ask for

    Rent, the deposit and whether bills are included are often left out of the post.

  • Slow back-and-forth

    Owners answer the same questions again and again over calls and WhatsApp.

  • Distance from campus is a guess

    It's hard to see which places are actually practical for getting to your university.

What we heard

We asked 18 students and 14 boarding owners before designing anything.

  • 13 of 18 students

    found it hard to check whether a room matched its photos or description.

  • 12 of 18 students

    said the rent, bills or deposit were unclear.

  • 8 of 18 students

    found a room was already reserved before they got in touch with the owner.

  • 11 of 14 owners

    found it difficult to reach university students.

Preliminary questionnaire run by the SmartBoard team through Google Forms in 2026. It is a small, self-selected sample, so we treat these as signals that shaped our design, not as figures for all students in Sri Lanka.

How it will work

One app. Two sides of the same door.

Students and owners sign in to the same app. It opens the view that matches your account.

Looking for a place

  1. Search near your campus

    Filter by area, budget and the facilities you need.

  2. Compare the details

    Photos, monthly rent, deposit, bills and house rules on one screen.

  3. Apply and follow up

    Send an application to the owner and see whether it's pending, accepted or declined.

Renting out rooms

  1. List your rooms

    Add photos, rent, deposit, facilities and rules once.

  2. Keep availability current

    Mark a room as taken so you stop getting calls about it.

  3. Review applications

    See who's interested and accept or decline in one place.

FAQ

Questions people ask us.

What is SmartBoard?

A mobile app for finding and listing boarding places in Sri Lanka. Students and young professionals can search and compare rooms; boarding owners can advertise rooms and handle applications.

Is it for students or landlords?

Both. Everyone uses the same app, and it shows you the tools that match your account type.

Who can use it?

We're designing it first for university students, then for young professionals who need affordable boarding, and for the owners who rent rooms to them.

Can I use it now?

Not yet. We're currently working on requirements, design and architecture, and development follows. The screens on this page are design previews.

How do I show interest?

Join the waitlist below. We'll only use your details to contact you about early access.

How will it work?

You'll search by area, budget and facilities, open a listing to see photos and full costs, and apply. The owner reviews your application, and you'll see its status in the app. Rent is arranged directly with the owner.
